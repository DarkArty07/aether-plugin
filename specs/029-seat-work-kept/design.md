# Design: a design seat's work is kept, and a seat that is still writing is not cut (issue #103)

- **Feature:** `specs/029-seat-work-kept`
- **Author:** the Lead (Opus 5.5), 2026-11-06. Revision 5: the Lead's fix of the two findings of the Judge's short check (stale lines of T1 and Verification, and the carry folder under `exams/`). Revision 4 took the third review's three findings (`revise`); revision 3 took the three findings of the second review, revision 2 the ten of the first.
- **Route:** short design. It touches the core of `/aether:design`: how a seat round ends, what it leaves, and who takes it next.

## The owner's words

Session of 2026-11-06. After issue #103 was explained (the design of a large card took hours because external seats were stopped at their time cap and the next pair started again), the owner said: «este si es importante porque no se puede estar perdiendo trabajo o sea tarde lo que tarde pero no se puede estar perdiendo trabajo si se pierde trabajo y se tiene que retomar desde cero pues evidentemente va a haber un problema». Then: «Lo puedes resolver de manera autónoma tu». The plan he approved in plan mode has the five criteria below and five assumptions; two of them settle the Judge's open questions:

- Assumption 3: «El Judge mantiene su tope total. No hay forma de ver su avance mientras revisa. Si se corta, pasa directo a Claude en lugar de a otro externo.» So C3 applies to the Judge too.
- Assumption 4: «El límite duro de cada harness (45 min para el Writer) se queda como red de seguridad. Si se llega a él, el avance se guarda y sigue Claude.» So the lane's hard timeout stays, as a safety net that loses no work.

## What happens today, read against `main` (0.33.0)

- `workflows/design.workflow.js` caps a seat round by its **total** time: `SEAT_CAP_SECONDS = { spec: 600, plan: 1500, design: 600 }`, measured with the `elapsedMs` that `aether-lane seat-wait` reports. At the cap the workflow runs `seat-stop`, counts the round as failed (`capped: true`) and asks `seat-start` for the next pair, up to `MAX_TRIES = 3`, then the native agent. The Examiner has no cap in the workflow. Every seat also has the lane table's hard timeout (`lib/lanes.js` `SEAT_TIMEOUTS = { judge: 1200, writer: 2700, examiner: 1800 }`, or the harness's own `timeouts`): `runHarness` (`lib/seats.js:562-580`) kills the harness group at `timeoutSeconds + KILL_GRACE_SECONDS`, and agy times itself out first with `--print-timeout ${timeoutSeconds}s` (`lib/harness/agy.js:119-120`); either way the round ends failed (exit 4) and the next pair is asked.
- **Writer:** since issue 68 its partial document is kept. `settlePartial` in `runRound` salvages the changed `spec.md` or `plan.md`/`tasks.md` of the round's worktree (`<main>/.aether/seats/<id>/wt`) into `partial.json` and an offer (`.aether/seats/carry/<session>/<feature>/<doc>`); the next external try applies it with `CONTINUATION_LINE`, and the native Writer gets it in its message (`partialForNative`). The exit-4 reply carries `partial` and `continued` (`lib/seats.js:954-957`). What is lost is time: each try that was cut spent its whole cap, and the next one re-reads the partial and can be cut again (spec 017: one plan revision took 74 minutes, two caps and a quota failure).
- **Examiner:** it writes only under `.aether-exam/` of its worktree. `settlePartial` returns at once for any seat but the Writer, its exit-4 reply carries no `partial`, and `seat-stop` names none, so the tests it wrote before a kill or a stop are **lost**.
- **Judge:** read-only; it answers only at the end. A cut Judge round keeps nothing, and no progress of it can be seen while it runs.
- Every harness adapter (`lib/harness/*.js`) prints its answer only at the end, so the round's stdout is no progress signal. The files a Writer or an Examiner writes in its worktree are.

## Criteria (the approved plan, made exact)

- **C1** The workflow no longer cuts a Writer or an Examiner on an external pair by a total time cap: once it has written, it is cut only after 10 minutes with no change to what it may write. Before its first write, the Writer keeps today's cap as a first-write cap, and the Examiner gets one of 25 minutes. The lane's hard timeout stays as the safety net (assumption 4).
- **C2** What a Writer or an Examiner wrote is not lost when its round is cut, times out or fails: it goes to whoever takes the round next, external or native.
- **C3** After a round of any seat is cut for time (the workflow's cap or inactivity, or the lane's hard timeout, from the runner's timer or the harness's own), the round goes straight to the native agent with what was written. A try that fails for any other reason still asks for the next pair.
- **C4** The result of `/aether:design` gives each stage's time and who took its rounds, for the Lead's brief.
- **C5** `npm test` and `npm run test:mod` pass. The existing tests that pin a behaviour this design changes are rewritten to the new behaviour, and only those: the ones that pin a cut round asking for the next pair (C3, listed in T2), the exact keys of a running `seat-wait` reply (item 1), and an Examiner exit 4 with no partial (item 4) (both listed in T1). Each task says in its report which tests it rewrote and why. New tests cover C1 to C4 with stubbed agents and spend no tokens.

Out, as the plan says: tasks that describe tests in words instead of code (it would undo A10; the owner decides it later), a limit of criteria per card, and a real design run to measure (the owner's rule against paid measurements).

## The reply shapes T1 and T2 share

- `seat-start`, on success: `{ started: true, id, at }`. `at` is the bin's `Date.now()`.
- `seat-wait`, still running: `{ done: false, elapsedMs, at }` for a Judge round (no `changedMs` key at all), and `{ done: false, elapsedMs, changedMs, at }` for a Writer or an Examiner round, with `changedMs` a number or `null` (defined in item 1).
- `seat-wait`, finished: `{ done: true, exitCode, value, durationMs, at }`. For exit 4, `value` is `runRound`'s failed reply. For the Writer it carries `continued` and `partial` as today (the list of `{ path, text }`, present only when not empty). For the Examiner it gains `continued` (boolean), `partialPaths` (the list of the kept files' paths, present only when not empty) and `partialDir` (the absolute path of the folder that holds them, under the data directory), and **never a text of the exam**. For every seat it gains `timedOut: true` when the round was cut by the lane's hard timeout (item 3), the key absent otherwise.
- `seat-stop`: `{ stopped, pair?, continued?, partial? }` for a Writer round as today; for an Examiner round `{ stopped, pair?, continued?, partialPaths?, partialDir? }`, with no text.
- `partial` and `partialPaths` paths are relative to the worktree root: `specs/NNN-x/spec.md`, `specs/NNN-x/plan.md`, `specs/NNN-x/tasks.md` for the Writer, `.aether-exam/<any relative path>` for the Examiner.
- **Where an Examiner's partial is kept, and why its text is in no reply.** The exam is hidden from the swarm (`finishExaminer` and `exam.store` move it out of the checkout, `lib/seats.js:737`, `lib/exam.js:65-66`), and every reply of a seat round is written to `<main>/.aether/seats/<id>/result.json` in the checkout (`writeResult`, `lib/seat-calls.js:140-143`) and journaled by the workflow. So the Examiner's kept files live only under the plugin's data directory, under the repository's key as the stored exams are (`exam.js:31-34`) but outside `exams/`, whose folders `exam.list` takes for features (`exam.js:81-97`): `<data dir>/exam-carry/<repoKey>/<session>/<NNN-x>/` for the offer, where `<NNN-x>` is `path.basename(feature)` as `exam.js` names a feature's folder, (the files at their nested relative paths, and its `round` digest). That folder is `partialDir`. No Examiner `partial.json` with texts is written anywhere; the replies carry paths only, and nothing under `.aether/seats/<id>/` holds a text of the exam. The next external Examiner try of the same round applies the offer from that folder; the native Examiner is told the folder (item 4) and reads the files itself. A round of the same session and feature with another digest clears the offer, as the Writer's does; an Examiner round that finishes clears it; and `exam.store` clears the Examiner offer of that feature for every session (`<data dir>/exam-carry/<repoKey>/*/<NNN-x>/`) once an exam is stored, with no change to its signature or to `aether-exam store`'s argv. The Writer's partial stays where it is today (its document is not secret).

The argv of `seat-start`, `seat-wait` and `seat-stop` does not change, so `hooks/ops-command.mjs` and its tests need no change.

## What changes

### 1. `seat-wait` reports when the round last wrote (T1, `lib/seat-calls.js`, `lib/seats.js`)

- **The baseline.** In `runRound`, after `git worktree add` and after an offer is applied (the Writer's today, the Examiner's from item 4), the runner writes `<seat dir>/baseline.json` atomically: `{ files: { "<rel path>": { size, mtimeMs } } }` for every file the seat may write that exists then (the Writer's `documentPaths(feature, doc)`, every regular file under `.aether-exam/`). A Judge round writes none.
- **`changedMs`.** `seat-wait` for a Writer or an Examiner round that still runs reads `baseline.json` and the worktree `<seat dir>/wt`. A file counts as changed when it may be written by the seat (the existing rule, `allowedFor`), is a regular file inside the worktree (the checks `textInside` uses: no symlink, no path that resolves outside), and is new since the baseline or differs from it in `size` or `mtimeMs`. `changedMs` is `at` minus the newest `mtimeMs` among changed files, never below 0; it is `null` when no file has changed, when `baseline.json` does not exist yet, or when the worktree is gone. The walk is `lstat` on the Writer's documents and a walk of `.aether-exam/` only, with no git. `allowedFor` and the inside-the-root check are exported from `lib/seats.js` and reused, not copied.
- **`at`** is added to every reply of `seat-start` and `seat-wait`.

### 2. The workflow cuts a Writer or an Examiner on inactivity (T2, `workflows/design.workflow.js`)

In `awaitSeat`, for seat `writer` or `examiner`, the round is stopped after a wait when either holds:

- `changedMs` is a number and `changedMs >= IDLE_SECONDS * 1000`, with `IDLE_SECONDS = 600`; the note says `no change for 10 min`;
- `changedMs` is `null` and `elapsedMs >= ` the first-write cap: `SEAT_CAP_SECONDS[doc]` for the Writer (600 for a spec, 1500 for a plan, as today), and `FIRST_WRITE_SECONDS = 1500` for the Examiner; the note says `no first write in <n> min`.

**Once written, always the inactivity rule.** The workflow keeps, per try, the elapsed time of the last change it saw (`elapsedMs - changedMs` from the last reply where `changedMs` was a number). Once a try has reported a number, a later `changedMs: null` (a file written and then deleted, or a worktree being removed) never brings back the first-write cap: the inactivity is then `elapsedMs` minus that kept time, against the same 10 minutes.

A Judge round keeps today's total cap, `SEAT_CAP_SECONDS[doc]`, and today's note. A reply of a Writer or an Examiner round with no `changedMs` key (a bin older than this change) is treated as a Judge's, so a mixed install keeps today's behaviour and never waits for ever.

**Waits.** Each wait stays `WAIT_SECONDS = 540` long, except the last wait before a known limit, which is sized to what is left of it, as today: the wait before a first-write cap or the Judge's cap; once a round has written, waits stay 540 s, so an idle round is seen within 540 s after its 10 minutes and the 12 waits of `MAX_WAITS` still span 108 minutes, more than any lane's hard timeout (45 minutes for the Writer, 30 for the Examiner by default). A round that is still running after `MAX_WAITS` is stopped as today.

The stopped round comes back as today (`{ id, note, capped: true, pair, durationMs, partial?, continued? }`).

### 3. A round cut for time goes straight to the native agent (T1 the flag, T2 the rule)

- **T1:** `runRound` sets `timedOut: true` in its failed reply when the round failed and either the runner's timer fired (the timer sets a flag before `killGroup`) or the harness exited after running for at least `timeoutSeconds - 30` seconds (agy's own `--print-timeout`, and any harness that times itself out). Nothing else sets it.
- **T2:** in `seatRound`, a try ends the external tries, with no next pair, when it was stopped by the workflow (`waited.capped === true`, for any seat) or its reply is exit 4 with `value.timedOut === true`. The native agent then takes the round with the partial (Writer and Examiner). A try that fails for any other reason (exit 1, exit 4 without `timedOut`, a start that failed) asks for the next pair as today, carrying the partial as today.

### 4. The Examiner's work is kept, as the Writer's is (T1 the runner, T2 the workflow)

- **T1:** `settlePartial` runs for the Examiner too. A round that did not finish salvages its changed (against the baseline), non-empty regular files under `.aether-exam/`, with the same inside-the-root checks as `salvagePartial`, into the Examiner's offer folder under the data directory (above), and records in `<seat dir>/partial-paths.json` only the list of their paths and the folder (`{ partialPaths, partialDir }`, no text). The Examiner's offer keeps each file at its path relative to the worktree root (nested folders kept; no `path.basename`), and loading it walks that folder; the Writer's offer and `offerDir` for `spec` and `plan` are unchanged. The next external Examiner try of the same round (same message digest) gets the files applied in its worktree and the line `An earlier examiner began this exam. Its partial tests are in the worktree. They may be incomplete or wrong. Continue from them after checking them against the spec and the plan.` `continued.json` is written for the Examiner too, so `seat-stop` names `continued`, `partialPaths` and `partialDir` for it, and its exit-4 reply carries them (the shapes above).
- **T2:** in `seatRound`, the Examiner's partial is kept as `{ partialPaths, partialDir }` from the last reply or stop that named it (a missing key keeps what is held, an empty list clears it, as the Writer's rule). It is accepted only when every path is under `.aether-exam/` with no `..` and `partialDir` is an absolute path with no `..`. The native Examiner's message then gets the line `An earlier examiner began this exam. Its partial tests are in <partialDir>: <the paths, one per line>. Read them there. They may be incomplete or wrong. Continue from them after checking them against the spec and the plan.` No text of the exam is in the workflow's messages to anyone but the native Examiner, in its `rounds`, its tries or its result.

### 5. Stage times in the result (T2)

The workflow keeps, per stage (`spec`, `plan`, `exam`, or the short design's document), the `at` of the stage's first `seat-start` reply as `startedAt`, and as `endedAt` the first `at` of the next stage, or, for the last stage, the last `at` it saw. The result gains `times`: a list of `{ stage, startedAt, endedAt, durationMs, providers, complete }`, where `providers` lists who took each round of the stage (the `provider` already in `rounds`) and `complete` is false when the stage's time ends at a reply that came before native work of that stage (the last stage ending with a native round): then `durationMs` is a lower bound. A stage with no dated reply (a bin older than this change) has `startedAt`, `endedAt` and `durationMs` `null`. The workflow calls `seat-start` with superdelegation off too (`--superdelegation off`), so with this change those stages are dated as well. The Lead's brief reads it: the RADAR "Rápido" line of `plugins/aether/skills/autonomous-runs/SKILL.md` gains "the `times` of each `/aether:design`". The bootstrap (`using-aether/SKILL.md`) is not touched, so its parts keep their size.

### 6. Docs

`docs/guide/design-seats.md` says what stops a round (inactivity or a first-write cap for the Writer and the Examiner, the total cap for the Judge, the lane's hard timeout for all), that a round cut for time goes straight to the native agent, and that the Examiner's partial travels like the Writer's. `docs/changelog.md` and the decision (A110) are written at integration.

## Verification

- `npm test` and `npm run test:mod` pass on the delivery branch with T1 and T2 merged.
- New workflow tests (the fake ops of `tests/workflow-design.test.js`): a Writer whose waits report `changedMs` under 600 000 past its old cap is not stopped; one that reports `changedMs >= 600000` is stopped with the inactivity note; one with `changedMs: null` is stopped at its first-write cap; an Examiner with `changedMs: null` is stopped at 1500 s; a Judge is stopped at its total cap; a reply without `changedMs` for a Writer keeps today's cap; a stopped round of each seat and an exit 4 with `value.timedOut` go straight to the native agent with no second `seat-start`; an exit 4 without `timedOut` still asks for the next pair; an Examiner's `partialPaths` and `partialDir` reach the native Examiner's message as a folder and paths, and no exam text is in `rounds`, tries or the result; `times` holds each stage with its duration, `complete` false for a last stage that ended native, and `null` times when no reply is dated.
- A workflow test: a try that reported `changedMs` as a number and then `null` is cut by inactivity, never by the first-write cap.
- New runner tests: an Examiner round killed mid-way writes no text of its tests anywhere under `.aether/seats/<id>/` (`result.json`, `partial-paths.json` and the rest hold paths only), its files are in `<data dir>/exam-carry/<repoKey>/<session>/<NNN-x>/` at their nested paths and `partial-paths.json` holds paths only, `exam.list` is unchanged by a carry folder, and `exam.store` of that feature clears that folder for every session; the baseline is written after the checkout and the offer; `seat-wait` reports `changedMs: null` for an unchanged checkout of an existing `spec.md`, a number after a write, and ignores files the seat may not write; `seat-stop` and the exit-4 reply of an Examiner round name its `partialPaths` and `partialDir`, and the next try of the same round receives its files; a harness killed by the runner's timer, or one that exits after `timeoutSeconds - 30` seconds, yields `timedOut: true`, and one that fails fast does not.
- Not verified: a real design run on external harnesses.

## Risks

- **A Writer that thinks for long before its first write** is still cut at its first-write cap, as today; its round goes native, as C3 says. After its first write it is not cut while it writes, up to the lane's hard timeout, where its partial goes native: no work is lost.
- **An offer applied after the checkout** is in the baseline, so it does not count as the new round's write.
- **Clock:** the worktree is on the machine of the bin, so `mtimeMs` and `Date.now()` share a clock.
- **Replay:** the workflow still reads no clock; `at` and `changedMs` come from ops replies, which the runtime replays.
